How to Use StandOut

A simple guide to help you get started.

01. Start with Missions

Choose a mission you want to complete and follow the instructions shown on the mission page.

02. Complete the Mission

Complete the required task. Make sure you understand the mission before marking it as complete.

03. Earn Your Reward

Once a mission is completed, your progress and rewards will be updated according to the app rules.

04. Keep Your Progress

Come back regularly, complete more missions, and continue building your progress.
